What Is BestClips?

BestClips is an AI-powered tool that takes any YouTube video link and returns the best moments inside it — complete with timestamps and shareable links.

Whether you're a content creator looking for viral clips, a viewer who wants to skip to the good parts, or an editor scouting footage — BestClips does the heavy lifting for you.

How Does The AI Work?

We use Claude (by Anthropic) — one of the most advanced AI models available — to analyze video context and identify moments that are likely to be funny, viral, or impactful.

Claude understands natural language deeply, so it doesn't just look for loud moments — it understands narrative flow, comedic timing, and emotional peaks to pick clips that actually matter.

Google Login & Privacy

We use Google Sign-In via Firebase for a secure, one-click login experience. We never see or store your Google password.

The only data we store is your Google display name, email, and profile photo — solely to personalize your experience. We do not share your data with third parties.

Deploy On GitHub Pages

BestClips is a fully static project — no backend required. You can host it for free on GitHub Pages in minutes.

Just push the three files (index.html, bestclips.html, about.html) to a GitHub repo, enable Pages from Settings → Pages → main branch, and your site is live.
